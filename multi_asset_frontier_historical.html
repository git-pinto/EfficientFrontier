Teaching takeaway

An asset need not lie on the efficient frontier by itself to be held in an efficient portfolio. Portfolio selection depends on covariances, not only expected return and individual risk. The highlighted allocations are conditional on the assumed inputs and constraints.

Constraints and interpretation

Default: long-only, fully invested portfolios. The chart displays a numerically approximated frontier using sampled feasible portfolios. With short selling enabled, weights are sampled between −50% and 150% subject to summing to 100%; the visualisation is illustrative, not an exact quadratic-programming solution.
